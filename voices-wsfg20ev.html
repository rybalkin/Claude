Hello, dear friends. Today we're going to talk about Siena... and I want to begin with a question.

What is Siena?

A museum? A pretty little town on a hill, with a famous horse race?

Actually... neither.

Siena is a city that once wanted to be the greatest city in Europe. And in a strange way, it lost everything. In thirteen forty-eight, the Black Death arrived. In a few months, more than half of the city died.

The great new cathedral was never finished. You can still see its empty walls, standing against the sky. A dream, frozen in stone.

And it is precisely because it lost, that it is so beautiful today.
