Siena Step by Step

From the station, a 14-minute introduction while you walk up into town. Then a loop of about 3.5 km with 21 stops, each 2–3 minutes long and never more than a few minutes apart, starting at Piazza Tolomei: the she-wolf, the oldest bank in the world, panforte, the Campo and the Palio, a pope who wrote a love story, a battle shouted from a tower, the cathedral that was never finished, St Catherine, and sunset at San Domenico. Each stop tells you exactly how to reach the next.

Before you start: turn the volume up, or share earbuds. Keep this page open with the screen on. Phones pause GPS when the browser is in the background.
