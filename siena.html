Siena Step by Step

A loop of about 3.5 km with 19 stops, never more than a few minutes apart: St Catherine, the water tunnels, a battle shouted from a tower, a cathedral that was never finished, the Campo and the Palio, panforte, and the she-wolf. Each stop tells you exactly how to reach the next. Giulia and James start talking as you arrive.

Before you start: turn the volume up, or share earbuds. Keep this page open with the screen on. Phones pause GPS when the browser is in the background.
