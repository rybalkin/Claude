Stops

    Good to know

    • Book ahead for the Accademia (David), the Uffizi and the Duomo dome climb. The dome runs on timed slots that sell out days in advance.
    • Churches ask for covered shoulders and knees. A light scarf in a bag solves it.
    • At the table, a small coperto (cover charge) per person is normal. Try bistecca alla fiorentina (sold by weight, meant to share), ribollita and a glass of Chianti Classico.
    • Paving is old stone and uneven. Comfortable shoes matter more than anything else on this page.
