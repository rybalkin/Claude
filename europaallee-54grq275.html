Europaallee: Tracks to Google

Fifteen one-minute stops along about 1.5 km, from the square behind Zürich HB to the Langstrasse: 150 years of railway and post, the Sihlpost of 1930, a failed mega-project, the teachers' college, UBS, Google's campus, and who actually owns the buildings Google rents. Every stop has a small 🔎 Find it. Each story starts by itself when you arrive.

Before you start: turn the volume up, or share earbuds. Keep this page open with the screen on. Phones pause GPS when the browser is in the background.
