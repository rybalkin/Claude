Dante's Quarter

About 800 m from the Ponte Vecchio to the Bargello: a murder over a broken engagement, a lucky bronze boar, chestnuts as ballots, Dante's church with letters to Beatrice, and Leonardo's sketch of a hanged traitor. Giulia and James tell the stories, and each one starts by itself when you arrive.
